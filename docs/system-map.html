1 · What a first-time voter does

Every screen exists to move someone from “I just turned 18” to “I know where to go and what to carry.” Progress is saved on the visitor's device and feeds the AI answers.

2 · The MERN system (branch fsd-mern)

React + Vite on VercelExpress on RenderMongoDB AtlasPostman-tested

The React client talks only to one Express API, which is backed by five Mongoose models and is the only part that calls Gemini. The lab's CRUD requirement is the Voting Plan.
