From eligible to voted

Steps 1 and 2 follow your progress on the checklist. Election dates are announced by the Election Commission of India.

  1. 1~6 months before polling

    Register to vote

    Submit Form 6 at voters.eci.gov.in. You need proof of age, address, and a passport-size photo.

    To do
  2. 2~2 weeks before polling

    Find your polling booth

    Search the electoral roll to confirm your name, serial number, and assigned polling station.

    To do
  3. 3Polling day

    Cast your vote

    Go to your assigned booth and get your identity verified. The Third Polling Officer presses the Ballot button on the Control Unit; you then press the blue button on the Ballot Unit for your chosen candidate and check your VVPAT slip (visible for about 7 seconds).

    ● Polling day
  4. 4After polling

    Results day

    Vote counting is conducted in the presence of candidates and their agents. VVPAT slips of 5 randomly selected polling stations in each Assembly Constituency (each Assembly segment in a Lok Sabha election) go through mandatory verification.
